Section 01

Overview

SafariQuest ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of all users who access our travel discovery platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use our services.

SafariQuest is operated from Nairobi, Kenya. We process personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and any other applicable data protection laws.

By using SafariQuest, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Platform.

Section 03

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following lawful purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To create and manage your account, display personalised content, and operate the Platform
  • Communications: To respond to enquiries, send booking confirmations, and important service notices
  • Improvement: To analyse usage patterns and improve our platform's features and performance
  • Safety & Security: To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Marketing: To send newsletters or travel deals, only with your explicit consent, which you may withdraw at any time
  • Legal Compliance: To meet our obligations under Kenyan law and respond to legal requests
Lawful Basis: We process your data based on: (a) your consent, (b) performance of a contract with you, (c) compliance with a legal obligation, or (d) our legitimate interests, where they do not override your rights.
Section 04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party providers for hosting, analytics, and email delivery. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Third-Party Listings: If you book or enquire through a listed hotel, restaurant, or tour operator, they will receive the information needed to fulfill that service.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by Kenyan law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, with notice to you.
  • With Your Consent: We may share data in other ways when you have given explicit consent.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion request
  • Reviews & Content: May be retained in anonymised form for platform integrity
  • Usage Logs: Up to 12 months
  • Financial Records: As required by Kenyan tax law (typically 7 years)

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Section 07

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Hashed and salted password storage
  • Access controls limiting who can view your data
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff training on data handling best practices

No system is perfectly secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

Section 08

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

👁️
Right to Access
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
✏️
Right to Rectification
Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
🗑️
Right to Erasure
Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
🚫
Right to Restrict
Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
📦
Data Portability
Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
✋
Right to Object
Object to processing of your data, including for direct marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@safariquest.com. We will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

SafariQuest is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@safariquest.com.
